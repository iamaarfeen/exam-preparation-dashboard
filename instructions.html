How to Use the Dashboard

Follow these simple steps to start tracking ICSE Class 10 preparation.

1

Download the sample Excel sheet

If you are new to the dashboard, download the sample workbook and use it as your template.

📥 Download Sample Excel Sheet
2

Enter your subjects and chapters

Keep one Excel sheet for each subject. Enter the chapter number and chapter name for every chapter.

3

Update the dates as preparation progresses

Enter the appropriate date when the student starts, completes, and revises a chapter.

Start Date When study of the chapter starts
Completion Date When the chapter is completed
Preparation Revision Date When the chapter is revised
4

Upload your Excel workbook

Return to the dashboard and upload your completed workbook. Your progress charts and forecast will update automatically.

Important: Enter dates as real Excel dates in the date columns, not as plain text.
